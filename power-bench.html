Fatigue power curve benchmark (#302)

Builds synthetic ride histories (no Strava data) at three sizes and computes the per-ride table (best power at every duration from 1 s to 5 h in four kJ bins) on the GPU and on the CPU. It checks the two tables match exactly, then times the GPU pass, the CPU pass and a filter sweep. Copy the JSON at the end into the issue.
